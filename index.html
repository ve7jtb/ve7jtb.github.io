CTAP 2.3+ Post-Quantum PIN/UV Auth Protocol 3: Architecture, Downgrade Integrity, Hierarchical PPUAT, and Encrypted Extensions

Defines a Post-Quantum PIN/UV Auth Protocol 3 (pinUvAuthProtocol = 3) for FIDO2 CTAP 2.3+ based on RFC 9180 HPKE (X-Wing, MLKEM768-P256, and ML-KEM-1024), addressing Harvest-Now-Decrypt-Later (HNDL) quantum threats and active bus MITM attacks while maintaining strict Relying Party unlinkability, zero extra Authenticator NVRAM, and 0-RTT key agreement caching.

Report Sections & Direct Deep Links
Section 1

Core PIN Protocol keyAgreement Issues & Post-Quantum HPKE Architecture

HNDL and active MITM vulnerabilities in CTAP 2.0–2.3 Protocols 1 & 2 (including unauthenticated Internal UV 0x0A), mapping §6.5.4 to RFC 9180 HPKE with KEM agility (0x2A: pinUvAuthKems), comparing HPKE authentication modes (mode_psk vs. mode_base) and DEM modes (AES-256-GCM vs. Export-Only), and 0-RTT caching of pk_batch against PPUAT_v3.

Section 2

Downgrade Issues & authenticatorGetInfo Integrity

Defending against KEM stripping, maxMsgSize squeezing, UV-to-PIN modality downgrades, and back-to-back protocol-translating relay MITMs without leaking fingerprinting bits into authenticatorData. Introduces 4-byte vendor root indexing (0x2B: batchAttestKid), client-reconstructed factory attestations (ReconstructedAttestation_v1), and 20-year security analysis of FN-DSA vs. stateful hash-based LMS.

Section 3

PPUAT Domain Separation Between PIN Protocols 2 and 3

One-way hierarchical derivation tree (PPUAT_v3 → PPUAT_v2 → K_id, K_css) requiring only a single 32-byte NVRAM root secret while creating a 2128 quantum preimage firewall between legacy P-256 Protocol 2 hosts and Protocol 3 HPKE mode_psk sessions, with 100% single-response wire compatibility for encIdentifier (0x19) and encCredStoreState (0x1E).

Section 4

Extensions Using Encryption (hmac-secret, largeBlobKey, credBlob, & sign)

Token-bound 1: keyAgreement elision in hmac-secret / hmac-secret-mc (saving 1.1–1.5 KB), encrypting largeBlobKey (0x07), deprecating credBlob (§12.5, originally designed for unreleased Kerberos KDC certificate hash pinning), and securing the WebAuthn sign (previewSign5) extension + ARKG on both the CTAP bus and end-to-end (RP <-> Authenticator via hybrid HPKE mode_psk).
